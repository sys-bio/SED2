---
id: SBML-0003
rule: "When the model's language is SBML, a numerical index on its '.model' output is not valid."
message: "{class} '{id}' references '{value}', but SBML models cannot be indexed numerically."
severity: error
status: proposed
check: handwritten
---

SBML defines no numbered collection of value-bearing elements for `.model` to
index into (see labels.md).  Labels are the only supported form.
